About Me

Carl Madelo is a Certified Duda Professional with 15+ years in web and design, specializing in Duda Flex and Duda 2.0. I design and build high‑performing, conversion‑focused websites aligned with both agency and client expectations.

I've delivered projects end‑to‑end, from full builds to platform migrations, creating scalable, responsive experiences across desktop, tablet, and mobile. Each project is grounded in clean design, optimized performance, and long‑term user experience.

Beyond design, I focus on speed, SEO‑ready structure, and long‑term scalability, ensuring intuitive layouts built to convert. I bring clear communication, fast turnaround, and meticulous attention to detail, supporting agencies with reliable, high‑quality execution.
